ODES is an independent engineering consulting firm specializing in design and engineering services, built on strong technical capability and extensive hands-on industry experience.

Founded by engineers with over 20 years of professional experience, our team has worked across automotive, mobility, and domestic appliance sectors, supporting both Tier-1 suppliers and OEMs.

We apply deep domain knowledge and practical engineering expertise to support the smooth and efficient development of even the most complex projects.

To be the most reliable, respected, and cost-effective engineering service provider, delivering exceptional quality with a strong commitment to time efficiency.
